Straight answers.

Is the online estimate really free?

Yes. Answer four quick questions, add photos if you like, and you get a price range plus the ROI numbers. No card and no obligation.

How do you work out ROI?

Every estimate shows three numbers: the projected value boost % for that kind of project, the estimated dollar lift to your home's value, and the value per dollar you spend. Prices come from current Long Island material and labor costs. These are projections, not guarantees.

What do I get from the $50 walk-around?

We walk your property in person and hand you an 8-card ROI matrix: the eight improvements with the highest return for your house, ranked and priced. If you book a project within 14 days, the $50 is credited toward it.

Will you tell me if a project isn't worth it?

Yes. If a job returns less than it costs, we'll say so and point you to the work that pays back more. That honesty is the whole point of Renov8ers.

Is the online price final?

No. Online numbers are estimates. The final price is confirmed after the contractor sees the job in person.
